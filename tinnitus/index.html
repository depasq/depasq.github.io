Tinnitus Notch Tuner

Find the pitch of your ringing, then listen to noise with that pitch filtered out. Everything runs on your device and nothing is uploaded.

Before you press play

Turn your device volume down first, then raise it slowly. The sound should be comfortable and sit under your tinnitus, never over it. If your ringing gets louder or anything hurts, stop.

Step 1

Find your pitch

Use headphones in a quiet room. Phone and laptop speakers can't reproduce high frequencies well. Move the slider until the sound matches your ringing.

Pick whichever is closer to what you hear.

Step 2

Listen to notched noise

Broadband noise with a band removed around your pitch. Many people play it for 30 to 60 minutes while reading or falling asleep.

About this

Notched noise is an experimental approach, and studies so far give mixed results. Treat it as something to try, not as a treatment.

Ringing that started after a loud show and is still there months later is worth a visit to an audiologist or ENT. A hearing test is useful background for any of this.
